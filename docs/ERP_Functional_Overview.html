1. What this system is

A web-based ERP built specifically for a flour mill (atta chakki / flour mill industry). It covers the full cycle: buying wheat at the gate (weighbridge slip), keeping wheat and bardana (sack) stock per godown, grinding wheat into atta / maida / fine / suji / bran, repacking, selling to dealers and bakeries, collecting money, and producing complete accounts (ledger, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow) plus 70+ reports.

The demo company in the system is Zafaran Flour Mills (ZFM) with a mill, head office and two sales depots. The product is being designed so it can be sold to many flour mills, with the old mill software (simple screens) as the reference for look and simplicity.

Key idea: the operator never types a purchase or sale voucher by hand. Saving an invoice automatically moves stock and posts the accounting voucher in one go. Only cash/bank payments, receipts and journal entries are typed manually.

3. Golden rules of the system

  1. Two documents only for purchase and sale. Order (optional, moves nothing) → Invoice (mandatory, the only thing that moves stock and posts accounts).
  2. Posted documents are never deleted. A mistake is corrected by Reverse, which creates a mirror entry (stock back out, voucher …-REV). Masters (suppliers, items, products, employees…) are never deleted, only deactivated.
  3. Three separate stock books, never mixed:
    • Mill Product Ledger — wheat, mill-owned bardana, atta/maida/bran etc. (per godown).
    • Party Bardana Ledger — sacks that belong to a supplier (party / returnable), tracked per supplier.
    • Stores Item Ledger — spare parts and consumables.
  4. Snapshots: values that drive figures (unit weight, rate, credit weight, yield) are saved on the document. Changing a master later does not change old documents or old reports.
  5. One number per document from its own series (PO-, WPI-, PI-, SAL-…). The auto voucher carries the same number as the document.
  6. Precision: weights keep 3 decimals (weighbridge), money 2 decimals. 1 mund = 40 kg.
  7. Reports read posted entries only. Drafts never show in statements.
  8. Cost of goods sold is periodic (owner's decision): one month-end Cost of Sales voucher from Opening + Purchases − Closing Stock, not per sale.

7. Purchase

Two invoice types: Wheat Purchase Invoice (mill goods: wheat and bardana, number WPI-) and Stores Purchase Invoice (spare parts etc., number PI-). Purchase Order is optional.

7.1 Purchase Order (PO-)

  • Supplier, date, godown, lines (item, qty, rate, unit). Currently used for stores items (the wheat/bardana section was removed from PO in the Oct review).
  • Lifecycle: Draft → Submitted → Partially Invoiced → Fully Invoiced (automatic) / Closed / Cancelled.
  • Approve action (with approval permission); Close asks a reason (supplier short, season over, quality rejected, over-ordered, accepted as final); Cancel asks a reason (entered in error, wrong supplier, duplicate, rate renegotiated, no longer required).
  • PO board shows Ordered / Received / Balance qty; opens on the "live" tab (submitted + partly invoiced).
  • A PO never touches stock or accounts.

7.2 Wheat Purchase Invoice (WPI-) — the gate slip

One flat page that mirrors the mill's paper slip. Sections:

SectionFields
HeaderV.No (auto), Date, Supplier (with balance chip), Godown, Vehicle No, Broker (a ledger account under "Brokers"), Brokerage per 100 kg, WHT per 40 kg, Manual remark / supplier invoice no.
Weight (KG)Party load & tare → Party weight; Mill load & tare → Mill weight; Selected weight = Party or Mill (choose). Difference shown.
DeductionsKatla, Impurities (khoot), Moisture, Sack weight deduction (bag weight × bags)
Bardana detailBag type (driven by Raw Bardana Linking of that wheat), number of bags, bag weight, ownership: Mill's own / Party / Returnable, rate (only mill's own bags are bought).
Stock detailsWheat item, Credit weight, Rate per mund, Amount
CarrierFreight amount, freight paid by mill (at gate) or by supplier, builty no, driver phone, transporter
Total billWheat amount + bardana − discount ± freight, brokerage, WHT → Net payable to supplier
Credit weight = Selected weight − Katla − Impurities − Moisture − Sack deduction (never below 0) Wheat amount = Credit weight ÷ 40 × Rate per mund Brokerage = Total credit weight ÷ 100 × Brokerage rate WHT = Total credit weight ÷ 40 × WHT rate Supplier payable = Total − WHT − (freight if mill paid it) − (brokerage if supplier bears it)

What happens on Save

  • Wheat stock + credit weight in the chosen godown (Mill Product Ledger).
  • Mill's own bags + in mill bardana stock; Party/Returnable bags + in that supplier's Party Bardana Ledger (mill is only holding them).
  • Auto voucher numbered same as invoice (see 12.3): Dr Wheat Stock / Bardana Stock, Dr Freight, Dr Brokerage, Cr Broker, Cr WHT Payable, Cr Supplier.
  • Rule: fewer than 20 kg per sack is refused; deductions bigger than load are refused; duplicate supplier invoice no. is refused.
  • Prints: Invoice (A4, weights + amounts + previous balance) and Voucher (gate slip).
  • Correction: Reverse (stock out of both bardana books and wheat, mirror voucher). No delete.

7.3 Stores Purchase Invoice (PI-)

  • Pick supplier → if the supplier has open POs, an order picker opens and lines are pulled (qty capped at pending, rate/tax defaulted, editable). Several POs can go on one invoice.
  • No open POs → empty line grid for direct entry.
  • Over-receipt against PO is allowed with a warning (recorded on the invoice).
  • Optional columns controlled from General Settings: sales tax, discount, freight.
  • On Save: stores stock + (weighted-average cost), item ledger, auto voucher, PO status advances.

7.4 Purchase Return (PR-)

  • Supplier → pick invoice → returnable lines (qty capped at what's left). Or Direct return (no invoice), rate defaults to that supplier's last purchase rate.
  • Returns stores lines (item ledger), wheat and mill bardana (mill ledger), party bardana (party bardana ledger).
  • Save Draft / Save & Post. Post = stock out + Dr Supplier / Cr Stock voucher. Reverse available.
  • Invoice detail shows "Returned qty".

7.5 Suppliers

  • Name, code, contact, city, NTN, opening balance + date, credit limit, credit period days, status.
  • Each supplier gets its own payable ledger account automatically.
  • Detail shows ledger; row actions: View · Edit · Ledger · Activate/Deactivate.
  • Supplier type (Wheat / Bardana / Stores) is derived from what was bought.

8. Sales

8.1 Sales Order (SO-) — optional

  • Customer, date, lines (mill products and/or stores items). Moves nothing.
  • Same lifecycle as PO: Submitted → Partially Invoiced → Fully Invoiced / Closed (with reason).

8.2 Sale Invoice (SAL-) and POS

  • Customer (balance chip, quick add), date, Godown (required), pay mode: Cash / Card / Online / Credit.
  • If the customer has open sales orders they are offered (optional).
  • Lines: sellable mill products (atta, maida, bran…) and stores items together in one picker; qty, rate (defaults from Product Rate), discount, tax (switchable).
  • Stock is checked against the chosen godown; it cannot go negative.
  • On post: product stock − from godown, auto voucher: Dr Cash (paid) / Dr Customer (balance), Dr Sales Discount, Cr Sales Revenue, Cr Sales Tax Payable.
  • POS screen: quick counter sale with thermal receipt.

8.3 Sale Return (SR-)

  • Against a sale: lines and qty returned; stock goes back into the same godown; voucher Dr Sales Returns / Cr Customer. Refund in cash is a separate payment voucher.

8.4 Customers & Customer Ledger

  • Same form as suppliers: opening balance + date, credit limit, credit period, city, contacts.
  • Own receivable account auto-created. Customer Ledger shows invoices, receipts, returns with running balance.

  • Quantity only — no accounting entry (costing is periodic via Cost of Sales). Editing reverses old movements and reposts, so nothing double counts.

10.2 Product Conversion (PC-)

  • Repacking: one product (qty × unit weight) consumed → one or more products produced, with packing bags. Example: loose maida into 50 kg bags; 40 kg bags into 20 kg bags. Quantity only.

11. Stores stock (spares & consumables)

  • Items & Stock: item name, code, category, UOM, rates, current stock and average cost. Import / export.
  • Item Categories, Units of Measure (incl. MUND), UOM Conversions.
  • Stock Adjustment (manual transaction): in / out with reason; posts an Inventory Adjustment voucher.
  • Item Ledger: two books selectable — Stores (item ledger) and Mill Products (product ledger) with running opening / closing.
  • Cost method: weighted average.

12. Accounts

12.1 Chart of Accounts

  • Tree: Assets / Liabilities / Capital / Revenue / Expenses → groups → leaf accounts. Only active leaf accounts can be posted.
  • Stock in Trade split into 4 accounts: Wheat Stock, Finished Goods, Bardana & Packing Stock, Stores & Spares.
  • Seeded accounts include Cash, Bank, Sales Revenue, Sales Discount, Sales Returns, COGS, Freight, Brokerage, Brokers (group), Withholding Tax Payable, Input/Output Sales Tax, Salaries & Wages, Salaries Payable, Salary Deductions Payable, Electricity & Fuel, Repairs, Office, Retained Earnings, Opening Balance Equity.
  • Each supplier / customer gets its own ledger account automatically.
  • Opening Balances screen; Fiscal Years with periods (fallback year starts 1 July).

12.4 Cost of Sales (month end, periodic)

Cost of Sales = Opening Stock + Purchases (net of returns) + Direct Expenses − Closing Stock Gross Profit = Sales − Sale Returns − Cost of Sales
  • Step 1: Products → Closing Stock, check/edit rates for the period end date.
  • Step 2: Reports → Owner → Cost of Sales → Post Voucher. One JV dated period end. Posting the same period again reverses the old one and reposts; overlapping periods refused.

12.5 Financial statements

  • Trial Balance: opening / period / closing Dr-Cr, flat or tree, integrity check banner.
  • Profit & Loss: Income, COGS, Gross Profit, Expenses, Net Operating Income, Other, Net Income. Variants: by month, comparison with previous year (change, % change), as % of income, detail.
  • Balance Sheet: as-of, comparison, by month, detail; "Current Year Profit" line; red row if out of balance.
  • Cash Flow: indirect and direct method; Changes in Equity.
  • Options: period presets (quarter, YTD, fiscal YTD…), columns by month/quarter/year, rows active / non-zero / all, ÷1000, without paisa, collapse levels. Every amount drills down to the account ledger.
  • Memorize: save current report settings by name (personal or shared).
  • Day Book in the mill's paper format (Bank Payment, Bank Received, Cash Payment, Cash Received, Wheat Purchase, Purchase, Credit Sale, Freight, WHT, Broker Commission, Returns, Journal) with B/F and C/F.

12.6 Period Close & Inventory Valuation

  • Period Close: preview then post closing journal to Retained Earnings (posted entries only).
  • Inventory Valuation: mill goods at Closing Stock rate + stores at average cost, one reconcile line per stock account vs ledger.

17. Technical summary

  • Python 3.12, Django 5.2 LTS, Django templates + HTMX + Alpine.js, Tailwind CSS (built locally, no CDN).
  • Database: PostgreSQL/SQLite locally, MySQL on live (cPanel hosting, HTTPS).
  • Modules (Django apps): core, accounts (login), access_control, configurations, organizations, hr, payroll, inventory (purchase, sales, stores), products, production, godowns, finance, portal (dashboard, reports).
  • Soft delete + audit fields (created/updated by/at) on every table; indexes on list and history queries.
  • Business logic in services (one transaction per posting); one component library for all screens with an automatic UI lint check.
  • Automated tests for inventory, finance reports, products, portal, settings.

18. Known gaps / open items (honest list)

  • Cost of goods sold is month-end (periodic), not per sale — product profit on a single invoice is not exact until month-end closing rates are set.
  • Grinding moves quantity only (no cost transfer entry); cost per bag is a report calculation.
  • Wheat Purchase Invoice cannot be linked to a PO (PO currently carries stores lines only).
  • Receipts are not allocated to specific invoices; aging is an allocation (newest first), not exact settlement.
  • Product sale stock check is against current stock, not stock as of sale date.
  • No separate weighbridge document — weighbridge reports read wheat slip lines; outbound (dispatch) weighment not recorded.
  • Sales have no broker / salesman commission fields.
  • Old header-only "pos-sales/new" draft form cannot add lines — to be retired.
  • Stores items have no reorder level and no godown dimension.
  • Payroll stores totals only; allowance breakdown report uses current salary items.
  • Item Categories / UOM screens are still two-pane, not the standard list board.
  • Latest accounts-review fixes (stock split into 4 accounts, payroll to GL, godown on sale, posted-only reports) are built locally; data repair command repair_books not yet run and not yet on live.
