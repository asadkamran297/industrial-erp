GRN Clearing — what it is for

When goods arrive without a bill, the value has to sit somewhere. It sits in GRN Clearing, a liability.

When the supplier's bill arrives, the clearing account is debited and the real payable is credited — the two net to zero.

Whatever is left in GRN Clearing is exactly the goods received that nobody has been billed for. Without it, payables are understated and there is no way to say by how much.
